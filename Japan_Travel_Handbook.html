Japan Travel Handbook

Itinerary Overview

  • Day 1 (April 15, 2024): Tokyo - Arrive, explore Asakusa, dinner at Izakaya
  • Day 2 (April 16, 2024): Tokyo - Visit Meiji Shrine, Kendo experience, Shibuya stroll
  • Day 3 (April 17, 2024): Kyoto - Travel via Shinkansen, Kinkaku-ji, tea ceremony
  • Day 4 (April 18, 2024): Kyoto - Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, Fushimi Inari Shrine
  • Day 5 (April 19, 2024): Nara - Visit Nara Park, Todai-ji Temple
  • Day 6 (April 20, 2024): Osaka - Osaka Castle, Dotonbori, evening proposal
  • Day 7 (April 21, 2024): Tokyo - Last-minute shopping, Tokyo Tower, departure

Essential Japanese Phrases

  • Hello: こんにちは (Konnichiwa)
  • Thank you: ありがとう (Arigatou)
  • Excuse me: すみません (Sumimasen)
  • Yes: はい (Hai)
  • No: いいえ (Iie)

Travel Tips

  • Purchase a Japan Rail Pass for convenient travel between cities.
  • Learn basic Japanese phrases to enhance your experience.
  • Respect local customs, especially in temples and shrines.
  • Try local foods and street food in each city.
