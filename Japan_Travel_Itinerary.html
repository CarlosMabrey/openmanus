# Japan Travel Itinerary (April 15-23) ## Day 1: Arrival in Tokyo (April 15) - **Flight from Seattle to Tokyo** - **Accommodation**: Check into a hotel in Shinjuku. - **Evening**: Explore Shinjuku, visit the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building for a panoramic view of the city. ## Day 2: Tokyo (April 16) - **Morning**: Visit Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa. - **Afternoon**: Explore Ueno Park and its museums. - **Evening**: Dinner in Akihabara, explore the anime and gaming culture. ## Day 3: Kyoto (April 17) - **Morning**: Take the Shinkansen (bullet train) to Kyoto. - **Afternoon**: Visit Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion). - **Evening**: Stroll through Gion, the geisha district. ## Day 4: Kyoto (April 18) - **Morning**: Participate in a traditional tea ceremony. - **Afternoon**: Visit Fushimi Inari Taisha (thousands of torii gates). - **Evening**: Dinner at a local izakaya. ## Day 5: Nara (April 19) - **Morning**: Day trip to Nara (visit Todai-ji Temple and Nara Park to see the deer). - **Afternoon**: Explore Kasuga-taisha Shrine. - **Evening**: Return to Kyoto. ## Day 6: Osaka (April 20) - **Morning**: Travel to Osaka, visit Osaka Castle. - **Afternoon**: Explore Dotonbori for street food. - **Evening**: Kendo experience in Osaka. ## Day 7: Proposal Day (April 21) - **Morning**: Travel to a scenic spot (e.g., Arashiyama Bamboo Grove). - **Proposal**: Plan a romantic proposal in the bamboo grove. - **Afternoon**: Relax and enjoy the surroundings. - **Evening**: Celebrate with dinner at a nice restaurant. ## Day 8: Return to Tokyo (April 22) - **Morning**: Travel back to Tokyo. - **Afternoon**: Last-minute shopping in Harajuku. - **Evening**: Prepare for departure. ## Day 9: Departure (April 23) - **Flight back to Seattle** --- ## Essential Japanese Phrases - Hello: こんにちは (Konnichiwa) - Thank you: ありがとう (Arigatou) - Excuse me: すみません (Sumimasen) - Yes: はい (Hai) - No: いいえ (Iie) ## Travel Tips - Use a Japan Rail Pass for unlimited travel on trains. - Learn basic Japanese phrases to enhance your experience. - Always carry cash, as not all places accept credit cards. ## Maps and Attractions -
