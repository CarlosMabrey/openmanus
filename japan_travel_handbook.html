Essential Japanese Phrases

  • Hello: Konnichiwa (こんにちは)
  • Thank you: Arigatou gozaimasu (ありがとうございます)
  • Excuse me: Sumimasen (すみません)
  • Where is...?: ... wa doko desu ka? (〜はどこですか？)
  • I don't understand: Wakarimasen (わかりません)

Travel Tips

  • Get a Japan Rail Pass for convenient and cost-effective travel
  • Carry cash as many places don't accept credit cards
  • Be respectful at temples and shrines
  • Try onsen (hot springs) for a relaxing experience
  • Use convenience stores for quick meals and ATMs
