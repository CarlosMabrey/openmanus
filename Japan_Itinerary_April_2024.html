# Japan 7-Day Itinerary (April 15-23, 2024) ## Day 1: Arrival in Tokyo (April 15) - **Flight from Seattle to Tokyo** - **Accommodation**: Check into a hotel in Shinjuku or Shibuya. - **Evening**: Explore Shinjuku, visit Omoide Yokocho for dinner. ## Day 2: Tokyo (April 16) - **Morning**: Visit Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa. - **Afternoon**: Explore Ueno Park and its museums. - **Evening**: Attend a Kendo experience in Tokyo (e.g., [Kendo Experience Tour](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g1066461-d11804685-Reviews-Kendo_Experience_Tour_Samurai_Trip-Taito_Tokyo_Tokyo_Prefecture_Kanto.html)). ## Day 3: Kyoto (April 17) - **Morning**: Take the Shinkansen (bullet train) to Kyoto. - **Afternoon**: Visit Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) and Ryoan-ji (Zen rock garden). - **Evening**: Attend a traditional tea ceremony ([Tea Ceremony Experience](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/tea-ceremony/)). ## Day 4: Nara (April 18) - **Morning**: Day trip to Nara (45 min from Kyoto). - **Activities**: Visit Todai-ji Temple and Nara Park to see the deer. - **Evening**: Return to Kyoto. ## Day 5: Kyoto (April 19) - **Morning**: Explore Fushimi Inari Taisha (thousands of torii gates). - **Afternoon**: Visit Arashiyama Bamboo Grove and Iwatayama Monkey Park. - **Evening**: Stroll through Gion district, possibly catch a Geisha performance. ## Day 6: Osaka (April 20) - **Morning**: Travel to Osaka (30 min from Kyoto). - **Activities**: Visit Osaka Castle and Dotonbori for street food. - **Evening**: Explore the nightlife in Namba. ## Day 7: Proposal Day (April 21) - **Morning**: Travel to a scenic spot for the proposal (e.g., Kiyomizu-dera Temple in Kyoto). - **Afternoon**: Celebrate with a special lunch. - **Evening**: Return to Tokyo for last-minute shopping and dinner. ## Day 8: Departure (April 22) - **Morning**: Last-minute sightseeing or shopping. - **Afternoon**: Flight back to Seattle. ## Travel Tips - **Essential Phrases**: - Hello: こんにちは (Konnichiwa) - Thank you: ありがとう (Arigatou) - Excuse me: すみません (Sumimasen) - **Transportation**: Get a Japan Rail Pass for unlimited travel on JR trains. - **Cash**: Many places in Japan still prefer cash, so have yen on hand. ## Special Proposal Locations - Kiyomizu-dera Temple (Kyoto) - Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden (Tokyo) - Arashiyama Bamboo Grove (Kyoto) ## Maps and Resources - [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps) - [Japan Travel Guide](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/)
