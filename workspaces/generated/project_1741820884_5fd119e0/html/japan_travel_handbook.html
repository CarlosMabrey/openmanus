Japan Travel Handbook

Essential Japanese Phrases

Hello: Konnichiwa (こんにちは)
Thank you: Arigatou gozaimasu (ありがとうございます)
Excuse me: Sumimasen (すみません)
Where is...?: ... wa doko desu ka? (〜はどこですか？)
How much?: Ikura desu ka? (いくらですか？)

Travel Tips

  • Get a Japan Rail Pass for convenient and cost-effective travel between cities.
  • Carry cash, as many places don't accept credit cards.
  • Be respectful in temples and shrines; follow local customs.
  • Try onsen (hot springs) for a relaxing cultural experience.
  • Use convenience stores for quick meals and ATM access.
